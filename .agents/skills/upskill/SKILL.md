# /upskill - Skill Gap Analysis & Learning Roadmap

Compares your candidate profile in `GEMINI.md` against tracked applications, recent postings in `seen_jobs.json`, or a specific job URL to identify technical growth areas.

---

## Workflow

1. Extract required and nice-to-have technologies from target roles.
2. Cross-reference with your verified competencies.
3. Produce a prioritized gap heatmap: High-impact (deal-maker skills), Moderate-impact (frequent bonuses), Low-impact.
4. Provide structured learning plans with web-researched tutorials, papers, and practice projects.
